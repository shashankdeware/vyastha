Shashank and Rahul have been close friends for years. While seeing how small businesses manage their day-to-day work, they noticed a common problem: many business owners still manage their accounts and daily operations on paper, while others rely on software that can be too expensive for a small business to afford.

They believed there had to be a simpler and more affordable way.

That idea led to Vyastha — a platform built to bring the essential parts of running a business into one place, from billing and inventory to payments, customers and business insights.

Our goal is simple: make useful business software affordable and accessible to the businesses that need it most.

Your business data belongs to you.

Vyastha is designed to keep your business information protected, private and accessible only through authorized access.

Cloud-Based Data Storage

Your data, securely stored in the cloud.

Vyastha stores business data on cloud infrastructure, so authorized users can reach their business information without depending on a single local device.

02

Privacy by Design

Your business information stays private.

Your business data is not meant to be publicly visible. Access is controlled through authentication and authorization, so only permitted users and systems can access the information required for their role.

03

Owner-Controlled Access

You decide who can access what.

Vyastha's role-based access is designed to prevent staff from opening areas outside their assigned permissions.
